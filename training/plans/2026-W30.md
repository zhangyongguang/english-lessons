# Weekly Speaking Training · 2026-W30

## Focus

This week introduces **word form (part of speech)** as the top new priority, keeps a **verb-complementation** target that the whole window shows is still shaky, and carries the **tense** focus forward from W29. The evidence window is 2026-W28 through 2026-W30; W30 contains the July 20-26 lessons.

## 1. Choose the right part of speech (noun / verb / adjective / adverb)

**Trigger:** reaching for a word that has related noun / verb / adjective / adverb forms — feedback, grow/growth, promote/promotion, serious/seriousness, easy/easily, tourism/tourist, compete/competitor, complex/complexity, talented/talent, embarrassing/embarrassed.

**Why now:** this is the single biggest new pattern in W30, with 15 errors across six lesson days. The three-week window contains 71 such errors across 15 days.

**Real patterns:**

- “It will feedback immediately.” → “It will give you feedback immediately.”
- “You have to help them growth.” → “You have to help them grow.”
- “It depends on the serious of the accident.” → “It depends on the seriousness of the accident.”
- “Our printers are convenient and easily to use.” → “Our printers are convenient and easy to use.”
- “A lot of tour guider changed their jobs.” → “A lot of tour guides changed their jobs.”

**Training cue:** before saying the word, decide its job in the sentence — thing (noun), action (verb), describing a thing (adjective), or describing an action (adverb) — then pick that form.

**Pass condition:** at least 8 correct first attempts out of 10 opportunities, one correct delayed retest, and correct use in two transfer contexts.

## 2. Use the correct verb form after another verb or preposition

**Trigger:** building a verb after another verb or after a preposition — start/help/ask + verb, get/be + past participle, “news/articles about ...” + -ing.

**Why now:** verb-form errors are the largest category in the window (76 errors across 17 days). W30 added 14 across all seven days: bare verbs where an infinitive or gerund is needed, missing linking verbs, and irregular participles.

**Real patterns:**

- “More and more companies start believe their data will help them.” → “... start to believe ...”
- “Exercise can help your body healthy.” → “... help your body become healthy.”
- “If you don't submit, you are catched by the policeman.” → “... you can be caught by the police.”
- “I read articles about big companies fire their employees.” → “... about big companies firing their employees.”
- “Someone lending me money bothers me.” → “If someone asks me to lend them money, that bothers me.”

**Training cue:** after start / help / ask / decide use `to` + verb; after a preposition use `-ing`; after get / be use the past participle (caught, not catched).

**Pass condition:** at least 8 correct first attempts out of 10 opportunities, one correct delayed retest, and correct use in two transfer contexts.

## 3. Keep finished events in the past and use present perfect for experience

**Trigger:** talking about a finished event (yesterday, when he started his job) or about life experience up to now (something he has or hasn't done or heard of).

**Why now:** carried forward from the W29 past-tense focus, which is still in learning. W30 added 11 errors across five days, including simple-past slips and past-perfect choices in unreal past conditions.

**Real patterns:**

- “I eat some meat and some vegetables.” → “I ate some meat and vegetables.”
- “When I started my job, I rent an apartment.” → “... I rented an apartment.”
- “I can't become a dentist because I didn't learn their skills.” → “... because I haven't learned their skills.”
- “I have ever done this many times.” → “I have done that many times.”

**Training cue:** finished-and-over → simple past; experience up to now → have / haven't + past participle (and no “ever” in statements).

**Pass condition:** at least 8 correct first attempts out of 10 opportunities, one correct delayed retest, and correct use in two transfer contexts.

## Five-session progression

| Session | Main purpose | Prompt support |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Free production, notice, self-correction, complete restatement | Moderate |
| Day 2 | Retrieve the same forms in unseen work and daily-life questions | Minimal |
| Day 3 | Transfer to jobs, travel, and technology topics | Minimal |
| Day 4 | Mix all three targets under follow-up pressure | None unless meaning fails |
| Day 5 | Unprompted assessment with unseen questions | None |

## Instructions for Chat Live

Use `training/live/current.md` as the complete 15–20 minute lesson prompt. Track only these three targets, count first attempts separately from prompted repairs, include delayed retrieval, and finish with the required Training report.

## Weekly decision rule

A target may enter spaced review only after at least 80% first-attempt accuracy, one delayed retest, and use in two contexts. Immediate repetition after hearing the answer does not count as mastery.
