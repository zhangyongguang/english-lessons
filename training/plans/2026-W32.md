# Weekly Speaking Training · 2026-W32

## Focus

This week trains three recurring foundations from the evidence window 2026-W30 through 2026-W32. The August 4 lesson supplies fresh examples for all three targets.

## 1. Verb form after another verb or preposition

**Trigger:** building a verb after `need`, `should`, `stop`, or `to`.

**Why now:** W32 added 9 verb-form errors; the three-week window contains 53 across 14 lesson days.

**Real patterns:**

- “I need ask some help.” → “I need to ask for help.”
- “You need to stop your work and had a rest.” → “You should stop working and have a rest.”
- “He is to study hard...” → “He needs to study hard...”

**Pass condition:** at least 8 correct first attempts out of 10, one correct delayed retest, and correct use in two transfer contexts.

## 2. Past-tense narrative

**Trigger:** describing completed events in work, meals, or personal experiences.

**Why now:** W32 added 10 tense errors; the three-week window contains 32 across 14 lesson days.

**Real patterns:**

- “I eat my dinner.” → “I ate my dinner.”
- “I eat some noodles.” → “I ate some noodles.”
- “The time went flying.” → “Time flies so fast.”

**Pass condition:** at least 8 correct first attempts out of 10, one correct delayed retest, and correct use in two transfer contexts.

## 3. Countable nouns and determiners

**Trigger:** choosing articles, plural forms, and quantifiers before nouns.

**Why now:** W32 added 8 plural, quantifier, and determiner errors; the three-week window contains 44 related errors across 14 lesson days.

**Real patterns:**

- “Get a permission.” → “Get permission.”
- “Different cities or place.” → “Different cities or places.”
- “I have many work.” → “I have a lot of work.”

**Pass condition:** at least 8 correct first attempts out of 10, one correct delayed retest, and correct use in two transfer contexts.

## Five-session progression

| Session | Main purpose | Prompt support |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Free production, notice, self-correction, complete restatement | Moderate |
| Day 2 | Retrieve the forms in unseen work and family questions | Minimal |
| Day 3 | Transfer to money, travel, and community topics | Minimal |
| Day 4 | Mix all three targets under follow-up pressure | None unless meaning fails |
| Day 5 | Unprompted assessment with unseen questions | None |

## Instructions for Chat Live

Use `training/live/current.md` as the complete 15–20 minute lesson prompt. Track only these three targets, count first attempts separately from prompted repairs, include delayed retrieval, and finish with the required Training report.

## Weekly decision rule

A target may enter spaced review only after at least 80% first-attempt accuracy, one delayed retest, and use in two contexts. Immediate repetition after hearing the answer does not count as mastery.
